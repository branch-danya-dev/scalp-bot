# Historical specification evidence

2024 NEARUSDT/XRPUSDT archives remain excluded from executable money labels and portfolio evaluation. Missing values are null, never zero. Tick size, quantity step, minimum quantity and minimum notional are separate fields. Trade-size minima cannot establish order constraints; observed precision can only disprove compatibility.

The exact unauthenticated Tardis endpoints, HTTP 401 and JSON bodies are preserved in `instrument-api-responses.json`. [Tardis documentation](https://docs.tardis.dev/api/instruments-metadata-api) requires Pro/Business and Bearer authorization for this metadata. Free daily CSV samples are separate. No subscription or key was acquired. Its change history guarantees completeness only for multiplier changes, so access alone would not prove every historical limit.

[Bybit's announcement](https://announcements.bybit.com/en/article/introducing-minimum-notional-value-requirement-for-derivatives-trading-blt0aea715040568196/) is dated 2024-03-19; web extraction exposed its title/date, direct HTML returned HTTP 403. [The official API channel](https://t.me/s/Bybit_API_Announcements/220) schedules USDT perpetual rollout from March 28 for Classic IDs ending 9 and Unified non-Pro; remaining Classic and Unified Pro from April 2. Closing orders are exempt. Its correction excludes inverse contracts and changes the error code to 110094. The date schedule does not establish each symbol's numeric minimum. No blanket 5 USDT value is applied to 2024.

PaperBroker's historical account cohort is unspecified. Therefore April 1 cannot be assigned a common Classic/Unified rule. By May 1 the published rollout covers the stated USDT perpetual cohorts, but numeric instrument restrictions remain unverified. All eight requested symbol/date combinations are retained in `historical_specification_matrix.csv`.

Alternative selected by available documentation: existing September 26 morning and noon raw captures contain dated instruments-info bootstrap results, including all four separate limits, for the actually activated symbols. Selection and chronological split were frozen in `docs/ml/experiments/pr58-documented-periods.json` before generating their labels. Both gzip tails are incomplete; only complete semantic records with verified content hashes and continuous hash links through recorded run_end are used. Teardown is not repaired and full original-scheduler replay is not certified. These additional archived periods are not independent calendar regimes or pristine research-unseen dates.

Contemporaneous September specifications are applied only to their own capture. They provide no evidence for 2024. The later two-hour development capture remains excluded from next-candidate fitting and evaluation.
